Pour apprendre l'anglais de A1 à B1 vite et bien, pas besoin de tout apprendre — il faut cibler ce qui revient tout le temps. Cette page fait exactement ça, avec un quiz en arabe et la prononciation (accent) pour chaque bonne réponse.

Lecture (accent) et écriture

Les 12 temps essentiels

Formation de base (forme affirmative), classés par groupe : Présent, Passé, Futur.

🟢 Present Simple

Sujet + verbe (+ s/es à he/she/it).
She works. They play.

🟢 Present Continuous

am/is/are + verbe+ing.
I am working. He is playing.

🟢 Present Perfect

have/has + participe passé.
She has worked. I have gone.

🟢 Present Perfect Continuous

have/has been + verbe+ing.
She has been working. I have been waiting.

🔵 Past Simple

Verbe régulier+ed ou forme irrégulière.
She worked. They went.

🔵 Past Continuous

was/were + verbe+ing.
I was working. They were playing.

🔵 Past Perfect

had + participe passé.
She had worked. I had gone.

🔵 Past Perfect Continuous

had been + verbe+ing.
She had been working. I had been waiting.

🟠 Future Simple

will + base, ou am/is/are going to + base.
She will work. I am going to work.

🟠 Future Continuous

will be + verbe+ing.
She will be working.

🟠 Future Perfect

will have + participe passé.
She will have worked.

🟠 Future Perfect Continuous

will have been + verbe+ing.
She will have been working.
